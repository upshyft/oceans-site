Your next home on the coast

You already know this coastline. This page is for the house that comes next: a different town, a different layout, or a move that has to work with the home you have now.

How it works

  1. Tell Oceans what you want to change, and which part of the South Bay you are considering.
  2. Look together in Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach, Redondo Beach, El Segundo, Torrance, and Palos Verdes.
  3. Decide what is worth a closer look. Talk through the next step before anyone writes an offer.

Decisions to make

  • Stay in your town, or move to another one on this coast.
  • What the next house needs to do that the current one does not.
  • Buy while you still own, sell before you buy, or line the two up. The move plan compares those choices.
  • When you want to begin. This page does not set a deadline.
