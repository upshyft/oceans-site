Which order you can carry

The decision is whether you can pay to own both houses, live in neither for a while, or hit two closings in one window. Emphasize one order. All three stay on the page.

Oceans Real Estate Group does not provide lending, legal, or tax advice, and does not guarantee a sale, a purchase, a date, or proceeds. We are not a lender or mortgage broker. We do not recommend a product, a rate, or a lender.

Buy first

You tie up the next house before the current one has sold. You may own both.

Carrying cost
Both loan payments, taxes, insurance, utilities, and any HOA dues, for as long as the overlap lasts. Ask the lender for that monthly number before you choose this.
If equity has to move early
A bridge, or a line against the house you still own, is a lender product. Ask what it costs if the sale closes late. We do not offer it.
Sale contingency
The purchase can depend on your sale. Some sellers refuse that, or take a higher price from a buyer who does not need it.
What slips the date
An appraisal under the price, or an inspection that reopens terms, lengthens the time you carry both.

Sell first

You learn the proceeds, then you buy. The open question is where you live in the gap, and what that costs.

Cleaner offer
Without a home-sale contingency, some sellers will prefer your offer. That is not a promise they will accept it.
The gap
Price a rental, a family stay, or a longer close. We do not provide housing and we do not promise a vacancy.
Ask the lender
What a completed sale changes about the next purchase, and what proof they want in hand before you write.

Coordinated closing

Both closings aimed at one window. One move, and little slack.

What breaks the match
An appraisal, an inspection, or a lender condition on either contract can move a date the other side cannot absorb.
Which contract depends
Ask that before you sign. If you want a legal read, it comes from your attorney. We are not that attorney.
Backup nights
Keep a few nights of housing even when the dates look aligned. Ask the lender what cash must be in the room if the sale funds late.

What we will evaluate

Which order the two specific houses can support, and the list to take to your lender: overlap cost, a bridge or equity line if you need one, and the cash required if a date slips. Keston has owned and managed short-term rentals, so holding costs are not a diagram. That still is not lending advice.
