Buying an investment

A plan for thinking about an income property in the South Bay before you tour. It does not project rent, occupancy, or a resale price, and it is not a promised return.

Next steps

  1. Write down how long you might hold a property, even if the answer is a range.
  2. Read the November 2026 Redfin figures already published on the homepage community cards. This plan does not add statistics.
  3. Choose one or two of the six communities to see first: Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach, Redondo Beach, El Segundo, Torrance, and Palos Verdes.
  4. List who would take care of the property if you are not nearby.
  5. Bring those notes to the investor form Oceans already uses.

Questions to prepare

  • What would you want a lender to confirm before you make an offer?
  • Would you live in the property for any part of the year?
  • Which city rules do you need to read yourself before assuming a rental is allowed? Oceans does not give legal advice.
  • What should your own CPA or attorney answer about how you would hold title? That is not a question for this page.

Decisions that usually come up

  • Whether you are comparing towns, or you already know the block you care about.
  • Whether management would be you, or someone you hire. Oceans is not offering property management here.
  • Whether the search waits until financing is clear, or you are still learning the coastline.
  • What would make you pause: repairs, rules, or a price your lender will not support.
