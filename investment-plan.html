Cash flow, or the later sale

The decision is whether the property has to pay you while you own it, or you are buying the future sale. Those are different bets. This page does not run either one for you.

Oceans Real Estate Group does not provide lending, legal, or tax advice, and does not guarantee rent, occupancy, appreciation, or a return. We are not a lender, and we do not recommend a loan product or a rate. City rental rules are yours to confirm.

Questions that change the bet

  1. Cash flow versus a later sale. Rent left after the loan payment, tax, insurance, and upkeep can be thin or negative while you wait on price. If you need the monthly leftover, do not underwrite the purchase on appreciation. We will not hand you a projected rent or a resale price.
  2. Rent assumptions and vacancy. If the rent in your head is a hope, the payment is still due. Ask who can support the rent figure, and what one vacant month does. Put a vacancy reserve aside before you call anything income.
  3. Maintenance reserve and capital improvements. A repair fund is not the same check as a roof or a kitchen. If the property needs a capital improvement to rent or to sell, price that before you count the return.
  4. If there is an HOA, read the documents, the reserves, and any special assessment. A thin reserve or an assessment coming due changes the cost to hold. We will look at those papers with you. We do not interpret them as your lawyer.
  5. Exit. Hold, sell, or a tax-deferred exchange. If you want to know whether an exchange fits, and when the clock starts, ask your tax adviser and, for the contract, your attorney. We do not design the exchange or promise the tax result.
