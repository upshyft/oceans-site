Communities

Local perspective across the South Bay.

Based in Hermosa Beach, Oceans Real Estate Group works hands-on across the South Bay — from Manhattan Beach to Palos Verdes — bringing deep, neighborhood-level knowledge of each community's character, trends, and hidden gems to every client we serve.

Manhattan Beach

Walk-to-the-sand living with a polished, upscale edge. The Sand Section puts you steps from the Strand and the Pier; the Hill Section trades a few blocks of distance for sweeping ocean and skyline views; and the leafy Tree Section offers a quieter, family-friendly pace minutes from downtown. Three distinct lifestyles, one tight-knit village.

Hermosa Beach

A compact, walkable beach town with an active, youthful energy. Pier Plaza and the Strand keep restaurants, shops, and the sand just minutes from home, while the Hill and Valley neighborhoods east of PCH offer more space and quieter streets without losing that easy beach-town access buyers love.
