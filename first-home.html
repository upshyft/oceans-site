My first coastal home

A plan for a first purchase along the South Bay. It prepares a conversation with Oceans Real Estate Group. It does not promise that a home will be available, or at any particular price.

Next steps

  1. Pick which of these communities you want to see in person: Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach, Redondo Beach, El Segundo, Torrance, and Palos Verdes.
  2. Ask a lender — not Oceans — what purchase price and cash you can use. Bring that answer with you.
  3. Write must-haves and things you can live without before you tour.
  4. Walk a few streets in daylight and again later, so parking and noise are not a surprise.
  5. Bring your questions to Oceans before anyone writes an offer.

What to prepare

  • Notes from your lender about range and cash. Oceans does not take loan applications.
  • Questions you will verify yourself about HOA rules, insurance, and coastal conditions, including with an inspector if you hire one.
  • Your timing, including when a current rental ends if you have one.
  • The communities you want to compare, even if the list is still two or three towns.

Decisions that usually come up

  • How close to the sand matters to you, versus a quieter block.
  • A smaller home that fits the lender's number, versus stretching for more space.
  • Whether you want one town, or you will look across more than one of the six communities.
  • What you still need a lender to explain before touring becomes a search.
